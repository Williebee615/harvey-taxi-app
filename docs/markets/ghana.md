# Ghana (pilot city: Accra): readiness checklist

Status: **test mode only. Not approved to operate.** Prepared 2026-10-10
from public sources found by web search; not legal advice. Every item needs
confirmation by Ghanaian counsel before launch.

## Verified (public sources, as reported)

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ride-hailing rules | DVLA guidelines (reported 2023) require private vehicles used for ride-hailing to be **registered for commercial use** (windscreen sticker), set a per-trip levy (GH¢1, subject to review) and require platforms to share trip data with DVLA. The DVLA's authority to levy was challenged. An earlier rule (about 2019): GH¢60 annual driver fee and six-monthly roadworthy checks. | [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2023/04/05/ghana-taxes-ridehailing/), [TechLabari](https://techlabari.com/dvla-imposes-mandatory-annual-fees-for-ride-hailing-cars-in-ghana/) |
| Data protection | Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843); Data Protection Commission. Controllers must **register** and renew every two years (within 20 days of starting business). | [Commission guidelines](https://dataprotection.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GUIDELINES-TO-DEMONSTRATE-DATA-PROTECTION-COMPLIANCE.pdf), [factsheet](https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Ghana-Factsheet.pdf) |
| SMS sign-in | Twilio lists Ghana alphanumeric sender IDs as **registration required**. | [Twilio sender ID support](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/223133767-International-support-for-Alphanumeric-Sender-ID) |
| Phone numbers | +233; mobile 2X and 5X (9 digits after +233). | Numbering plan (as configured) |

## Unresolved

1. **Current ride-hailing rules:** whether the 2023 DVLA guidelines are in force, the levy's status, and any 2025–2026 changes. Nothing Accra-specific was found.
2. **Company presence and tax:** GRA registration, VAT/NHIL/levies on fares and commissions.
3. **Pricing:** local fare levels (current values are illustrative).
4. **Payments:** mobile money (for example MTN MoMo) through a licensed provider: onboarding, fees, settlement. Cash process. **Not enabled until known.**
5. **Emergency numbers:** 112 is listed; also 191 police, 192 fire, 193 ambulance, and 999 in some lists. Confirm from mobiles in Accra.
6. **Driver documents and expiry:** confirm the list (Ghana Card, licence category, police report, DVLA commercial registration, roadworthy certificate, insurance, ride-hailing sticker).
7. **Identity and background checks:** coverage for Ghana Card and police reports.
8. **Privacy:** the Act's cross-border position is unclear (sources disagree); a Ghana privacy notice; AI transfers (AI off until assessed); whether a 2025 Data Protection Bill has passed.
9. **Maps:** address quality and routing in Accra.
10. **Insurance.**
