## 3. Text-message consent retention (new wording, PR #197)
PR #197 adds this sentence to Privacy section 15 (Text Messages):

> "HTAF keeps a record of your text-message choices (your opt-in, with its date, source, and wording version, and any opt-out) so that it can honor them and show that consent was given."

It does not state a period. **Proposed alternatives**, for the owner to pick one or give another:
- **A (as drafted, no fixed period):** keep the sentence above.
- **B (fixed period after last activity):** "HTAF keeps a record of your text-message choices, including any opt-out, for 4 years after your last text-message activity with HTAF, so that it can honor them and show that consent was given."
- **C (tied to the application schedule):** "HTAF keeps a record of your text-message choices, including any opt-out, for as long as it keeps the related application under Section 10, and for at least 4 years after your last text-message activity."

Notes on the alternatives:
- The 4-year figure is a common choice because of the four-year limitations period generally applied to federal TCPA claims. Counsel should confirm it.
- Opt-outs should not be deleted while HTAF might still text that number, because deleting one could cause an unwanted text.

**Needed from owner/counsel:** A, B, C or other. If B or C, a matching line is also added to Section 10's retention schedule.
