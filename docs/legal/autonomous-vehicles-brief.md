# Brief for Tennessee transportation counsel and an insurance broker: autonomous vehicles

**Prepared:** 2026-10-17 (UTC), at the owner's request. **For:** Harvey Taxi Service LLC (Nashville, Tennessee). **Status:** questions for professional advice. This brief is not legal advice and states no conclusions.

**Labels:**
- **[Fact]**: Harvey Taxi's own current operations.
- **[To confirm]**: a law or rule summarized from secondary sources, which counsel should check against the official text.
- **[Question]**: what we need answered.

---

## 1. Who we are and what we do today

- **[Fact]** Harvey Taxi Service LLC runs a ride-hailing platform in early access in Nashville. Every trip has a licensed human driver who is an independent contractor. Riders pay by card through Stripe. There are a rider app, a driver app and a website (harveytaxiservice.com).
- **[Fact]** Harvey Taxi doesn't operate, own or dispatch any autonomous vehicle today. As of 17 October 2026, the apps show autonomous rides as "coming soon", with an email waitlist. The server refuses any autonomous ride request.
- **[Fact]** A separate nonprofit, the Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation (HTAF), is outside the scope of this brief unless counsel advises otherwise.

## 2. What the owner wants to do

1. **Owner-operated autonomous vehicles.** Drivers who own a vehicle with an automated driving system send it out, with no one inside, to complete Harvey Taxi trips. They earn income as their own business.
2. **A Harvey Taxi autonomous fleet,** owned or leased, operated under Harvey Taxi's name.
3. **Probable first step:** a dispatch partnership with an established autonomous-vehicle operator that supplies certified vehicles, permits and insurance, similar to the Uber and Waymo model.

## 4. Questions for counsel

**A. Status of owner-operated autonomous vehicles**
1. Can a private owner lawfully have a Tennessee-registered automated vehicle carry paying passengers with nobody inside? What must the owner do: registration, identification as automated, insurance, and any business licence?
2. Who is the legal "operator" or responsible party for a driverless trip dispatched through Harvey Taxi: the owner, the system's manufacturer, Harvey Taxi, or a combination? How does § 55-30-106 assign liability?
3. Is the owner still a "TNC driver" under Tennessee law when nobody drives? If not, which framework applies (a TNC, a for-hire carrier, or something else)?
4. What contract terms with owners are needed? For example: requiring the manufacturer's authorization for unsupervised commercial use, maintenance and inspection, data access, indemnity, and suspension rights.
5. Can Harvey Taxi lawfully accept vehicles whose maker labels the system "supervised", that is, needing an attentive driver? We assume not, for driverless trips.

**B. A Harvey Taxi fleet, and partnerships**

6. What licences, registrations or filings would Harvey Taxi need as owner or operator of a fleet in Tennessee? Is any state approval or notice required before starting?
7. In a partnership with an autonomous-vehicle operator, how should liability, insurance and incident handling be divided? What should we require in the partner's documents?

**C. Riders and safety**

8. What must riders be told before booking a driverless trip, and how should their consent be recorded?
9. Rules for minors: can unaccompanied minors ride? What age policy should apply to driverless trips, and to all trips?
10. What incident-reporting duties apply after a driverless crash or a safety event, at state and federal level, and within what deadlines?
11. Accessibility (ADA): what duties apply to an autonomous service, including wheelchair-accessible vehicles and service animals?
12. What data, privacy and record-keeping rules apply to vehicle camera and sensor data and trip data?

**D. Marketing and the app stores**

13. What may we say publicly about plans for autonomous rides before launch, to avoid misleading-advertising risk?
14. Do the waitlist and the vehicle-owner interest list (by email) raise any consent or privacy-policy issues?

**E. Fleet management (human-driven, planned first; added 2026-10-22)**

The plan is in `docs/fleet-management-plan.md`. A verified Harvey Taxi driver (the "fleet owner") registers vehicles and invites other verified drivers to drive them. Harvey Taxi records an agreed share of each trip's earnings for the fleet owner, but moves no money in the first phase.

15. **Contractor status.** If a fleet owner takes an agreed share of another driver's trip earnings, does that create an employment relationship between them, or affect Harvey Taxi's position on contractor status? What contract terms reduce that risk?
16. **Licensing.** Does a fleet owner need a business licence, permit or registration in Tennessee or Metro Nashville to put vehicles they own on a TNC platform?
17. **Regulatory status.** Is a fleet owner a "TNC driver", a vehicle lessor, or something else under Tennessee law? Does the TNC chapter allow drivers to use vehicles they don't own?
18. **Data sharing.** What may a fleet owner see about the drivers using their vehicles (trip counts, earnings, online status, vehicle location)? What consent wording is needed?
19. **Share cap.** Should Harvey Taxi cap or set the fleet owner's share, or only record what the two parties agree?

## 5. Questions for the insurance broker

1. Which carriers write commercial liability for driverless vehicles in Tennessee? What limits are typical, and is $5 million needed or market standard?
2. How would cover work for an owner's vehicle dispatched by Harvey Taxi, in each of these periods: logged on and waiting, on the way to a pickup, and during the trip? Can our TNC policy be extended to it?
3. What would a small pilot fleet need for liability, physical damage, cyber, product liability and excess cover? What are indicative premiums?
4. What will underwriters ask for? For example: system safety data, remote-monitoring procedures, incident-response plans and operating limits.
5. Does an AV operator partnership change Harvey Taxi's own insurance needs?

6. **Fleet vehicles.** Under § 55-12-141, whose policy must cover a fleet vehicle driven by someone who doesn't own it, in each period (logged on, en route, on trip)? Can the TNC policy cover it, or must the fleet owner's commercial policy?
7. What do insurers require from a small fleet owner (commercial auto, hired and non-owned auto, named drivers)? Is a vehicle inspection required?
