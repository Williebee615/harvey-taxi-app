Programs

Four ways we put someone in a seat.

Each program is funded separately and reported separately, so a donor or grantmaker can see exactly which trips their support paid for.

Healthcare access

Non-emergency medical transportation

Scheduled and recurring trips to dialysis, oncology, behavioral health, and follow-up care — including wheelchair-accessible service.

Employment access

Job access and reverse commute

Rides to shifts that start before or end after fixed-route service, and to worksites outside the transit network.

Essential needs

Groceries and social services

Trips to food access points, benefits appointments, pharmacies, and community programs.

Community

Partner and referral rides

Trips requested on a client's behalf by clinics, case managers, shelters, and community organizations.

How it works

We don't run a garage. We run the dispatch.

The Foundation operates a mobility-management model: instead of buying and maintaining a fleet, we coordinate trips through vetted transportation network providers and contracted drivers on the Code Blue Platform. More of every dollar reaches the road, and capacity scales with need instead of with parking spaces.

01

A trip is requested

By the rider, a family member, or a partner clinic or case manager.

02

Eligibility is confirmed

We verify the trip fits a funded program and the rider's access needs — including accessible vehicle requirements.

03

Dispatch assigns the ride

The Code Blue Platform routes the trip to a background-checked driver or a contracted network provider.

04

The trip is subsidized and recorded

The rider pays nothing or a reduced fare. The trip is logged for program reporting and funder accountability.
