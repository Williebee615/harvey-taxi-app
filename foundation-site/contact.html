Request a ride

Need a ride, or arranging one for someone else?

Tell us the appointment, the address, and the access needs. We will confirm whether the trip can be covered before you have to worry about it. Requests can be made by riders, family members, or referring organizations.

Email a ride request

  • Give as much notice as you can. Recurring treatment schedules are easiest for us to cover.
  • Tell us about access needs. Wheelchair, walker, oxygen, service animal, or a companion riding along.
  • Referring a client? Case managers and clinic staff can request on a client's behalf.
  • Service area. Nashville and Davidson County, with select trips in surrounding counties.
