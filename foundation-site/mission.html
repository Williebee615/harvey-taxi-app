Our mission

Transportation is the barrier that hides behind every other barrier.

People do not miss treatment because they do not want care. They miss it because the ride fell through.

Mission statement

Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation exists to remove the transportation barrier that keeps seniors, people with disabilities, and low-income families in Middle Tennessee from reaching healthcare, employment, education, and essential needs.

Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation is a Nashville nonprofit that pays for and coordinates the trips our neighbors cannot otherwise make — to healthcare, to work, to school, and to food. We do this by funding rides directly and by coordinating a network of vetted drivers and transportation providers, so that a lack of a car, a license, or a bus route within walking distance never becomes the reason someone misses an appointment, a shift, or a meal.

Who we serve

Neighbors for whom a ride is not guaranteed.

Who we serve

Seniors aging in place

Older adults living independently without a vehicle, a driver's license, or family close enough to help.

Who we serve

People with disabilities

Riders who need accessible vehicles, door-through-door assistance, or trips paratransit cannot schedule in time.

Who we serve

Low-income working families

Households where a broken-down car means a lost shift, and a lost shift means a lost job.

Accountability

A registered public charity, reporting on every trip.

Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, EIN 41-5115030, based in Nashville, Tennessee. Each program is funded and reported separately, so donors and grantmakers can see exactly which trips their support paid for.
