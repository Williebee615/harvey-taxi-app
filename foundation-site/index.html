Our mission

Transportation is the barrier that hides behind every other barrier.

People do not miss treatment because they do not want care. They miss it because the ride fell through. Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation is a Nashville nonprofit that pays for and coordinates the trips our neighbors cannot otherwise make — to healthcare, to work, to school, and to food.

Mission statement

Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation exists to remove the transportation barrier that keeps seniors, people with disabilities, and low-income families in Middle Tennessee from reaching healthcare, employment, education, and essential needs.

Programs

Four ways we put someone in a seat.

Each program is funded separately and reported separately, so a donor or grantmaker can see exactly which trips their support paid for.

Healthcare access

Non-emergency medical transportation

Scheduled and recurring trips to dialysis, oncology, behavioral health, and follow-up care — including wheelchair-accessible service.

Employment access

Job access and reverse commute

Rides to shifts that start before or end after fixed-route service, and to worksites outside the transit network.
