Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

At Harvey Taxi Service LLC, we are committed to keeping our platform secure, fair, and transparent. This Privacy Policy outlines exactly how we manage, utilize, and secure your data across our rideshare infrastructure, mobile web portals, and matching APIs.

1. Spatial Telemetry & Location Monitoring

To deliver instantaneous dispatch calculations and track active passenger trips, we collect and monitor accurate hardware location parameters:

  • Real-Time Tracking: With your permission, we trace device GPS coordinates to show available drivers nearby and safely monitor active rides.
  • Background Positioning: Driver clients stream continuous background location updates to ensure optimal route distribution. For passengers, tracking metrics are capped when the trip lifecycle finishes.
  • Masked Data Layers: Raw coordinates are safely parsed inside backend container structures while staying hidden inside client-facing user interfaces.

2. Information We Collect

We process standard account information required to establish secure rideshare operations:

  • Rider Profiles: System ID strings, first names, and profile attributes needed for passenger validation and matching protocols.
  • Contact Linking: Phone numbers and verified email endpoints utilized for automated system dispatch messages, support updates, and secure receipts.
  • Trip Configurations: Metadata details surrounding pick-up points, drop-off paths, manifest comments, and selected fleet choices.
Secure Transaction Architecture All payment tokens clear directly through sandboxed third-party networks (like Stripe or Apple Pay). Harvey Taxi Service LLC does not log, process, or keep your primary credit card numbers or banking authentication tokens on local hardware systems.

3. Data Usage Framework

Collected user metrics are only used to support direct network operations:

  • Computing route distances, trip runtimes, and gross fare matrix calculations.
  • Enabling secure proxy communications between drivers and riders while preserving contact privacy.
  • Running account status validation checks to protect network safety and prevent booking fraud.

4. Sharing with Third-Party Networks

We do not broker, trade, or expose your personal trip histories or profile configurations to outside advertising companies. Data parameters are strictly distributed to core operational tools (like mapping APIs, SMS dispatch alerts, and secure transaction systems) required to run the service.

5. Your Rights & Profiles

Users have full access control over their accounts and can request an export of their trip logs, make profile updates, or completely delete their account files from our servers by connecting with the support team.

6. Framework Modifications

Harvey Taxi Service LLC reserves the authority to issue regular updates to this policy framework to maintain compliance with municipal regulations and technology standards. Using the portal signifies active consent to current rules.

7. Medical & Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)

When Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation (HTAF) coordinates transportation for medical appointments referred by a healthcare partner, Harvey Taxi Service LLC and HTAF utilize enterprise-grade infrastructure (including Lyft Business) and comply with applicable HIPAA standards for any protected health information (PHI) shared with us by a healthcare partner in connection with such referrals. PHI received in this context is used solely to arrange and complete the requested transportation.

Where required, Harvey Taxi Service LLC and/or HTAF will execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with a referring healthcare partner before receiving or transmitting PHI in connection with medical transportation coordination.
