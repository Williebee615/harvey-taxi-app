Privacy Policy

Last Compliance Sync: May 2026

At Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation, we recognize the paramount importance of data security and transparency. This Privacy Policy details the exact framework governing how our non-profit structural containers gather, protect, process, and handle data layers generated by our visitors, donors, and participant networks.

1. Information Collected Across Infrastructure Nodes

We only log informational parameters necessary to fulfill community transportation assistance services and maintain operational compliance:

  • Identity Parameters: Alphanumeric identification frameworks, structural user tokens, and names provided voluntarily during account onboarding.
  • Contact Infrastructure: Valid email strings and authenticated hardware telephone numbers used for logistical support updates.
  • Spatial Navigation Metrics: Hardware GPS coordinates, geocoded signatures, and text-based addresses submitted via booking terminals to optimize live dispatch visualization.

2. Financial Handshake & Donor Processing Operations

To safeguard capital routing pipelines, all transactions execute inside sandboxed third-party clearing frameworks. We do not store raw primary account numbers or financial authentication secrets on our local servers.

Personal details shared during the donor lifecycle are subjected to strict non-profit privacy preservation matrices. PayPal processes the gross total securely without sharing critical payment asset details with our application backend layer.

3. Utilization of Data Matrices

Gathered data streams are exclusively utilized inside the following operational scopes:

  • Calculating spatial ride matrices and trip runtime metrics via mapping providers.
  • Verifying rider identity statuses with compliance gates before allocating non-profit assistance funds.
  • Distributing direct service confirmations, tax-deductible donor sheets, and technical alerts.

4. Third-Party Data Disclosures & Security Enclaves

We do not lease, trade, or monetize transactional records or profile configurations to external marketing conglomerates. Information layers are exclusively distributed to trusted system vendors (such as map libraries or cloud microservices) essential for operational integrity.

Data packets remain secured using active socket encryption layers during raw network transmission loops to prevent interception parameters.

5. Strategic User Rights & Global Alignment

Account holders retain the structural privilege to request full diagnostic dumps of stored metrics, register data modification updates, or initiate complete purge protocols of their profile logs from our production containers by interfacing directly through standard support endpoints.
