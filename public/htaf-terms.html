1. About HTAF and These Terms

HTAF helps eligible individuals access transportation for healthcare, employment, education, and other essential needs. HTAF is a charity, not a transportation company: it does not operate vehicles, charge fares, or run a ride-hailing service.

2. HTAF, Harvey Taxi Service LLC, and Transportation Providers

HTAF arranges approved trips through independent transportation providers and program partners. Harvey Taxi Service LLC is a separate for-profit company and is not HTAF. It may provide or coordinate an approved trip only when selected to do so, and it may receive trip information only in that case. Transportation providers are responsible for operating their own vehicles and services.

3. Applications and Eligibility

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, funding, transportation, or scheduling of any kind.

Each application is reviewed by HTAF personnel based on eligibility criteria, verification, available funding, and HTAF's capacity at the time of review. HTAF may approve, deny, delay, or request more information about any application, and may be unable to help an otherwise eligible applicant when funding or transportation is not available. HTAF does not charge a fee to apply.

5. How Transportation Assistance Works

If an application is approved, HTAF will work with you to arrange the approved trip with an independent transportation provider. Pickup times, routes, and availability depend on the provider. HTAF shares only the minimum trip information needed to arrange the trip, as described in the Privacy Policy.
