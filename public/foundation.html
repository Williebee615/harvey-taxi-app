About HTAF

Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation (HTAF) is a 501(c)(3) public charity established to remove transportation barriers that keep individuals and families from reaching the healthcare, employment, education, and community resources they need. Transportation is one of the most common — and most overlooked — obstacles standing between Tennesseans and the services that support their health and independence. HTAF exists to close that gap.

HTAF serves eligible Tennessee residents statewide, with assistance programs covering medical appointments, employment access, education, veterans, seniors, individuals with disabilities, and essential community and emergency transportation needs. Every application is reviewed individually against program eligibility, supporting documentation, and available funding — submitting an application is not a guarantee of assistance, but every request receives real review.

As a registered 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 41-5115030), HTAF relies on donations and community partnerships to fund the transportation assistance it provides.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does submitting an application guarantee assistance? No. Assistance depends on eligibility, documentation, transportation availability, and funding resources.
Who can apply? Tennessee residents seeking transportation support for essential needs.
What documentation may be required? Appointment confirmations, TennCare records, employment verification, disability documentation, school enrollment, and other supporting records.
