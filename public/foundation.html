Why Transportation Matters

Transportation is often the missing link between people and opportunity. Reliable mobility affects healthcare outcomes, employment success, educational achievement, and overall quality of life.

Healthcare Transportation barriers often contribute to missed medical appointments and delayed care.
Employment Reliable transportation supports workforce participation and economic stability.
Education Mobility access helps students participate consistently in educational opportunities.
Community Transportation strengthens community engagement and access to support resources.
