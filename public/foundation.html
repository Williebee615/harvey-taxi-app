Transportation Assistance Application Portal

Submit a transportation assistance request directly to Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation. Applications are reviewed based on transportation need, eligibility requirements, funding availability, and supporting documentation.

Medical appointments, TennCare documents, disability verification, veteran paperwork, employment verification, school schedules, referral letters, or other supporting records.
Applicant Certification By submitting this application, you certify that all information provided is accurate, truthful, and complete to the best of your knowledge. Submission does not guarantee transportation assistance and all requests remain subject to review, eligibility, documentation verification, and funding availability.

Application Review Process

Every application follows a structured review workflow to ensure fairness, accountability, and responsible use of transportation resources.

Step 1 Submit transportation assistance application and supporting documents.
Step 2 HTAF reviews transportation need, eligibility requirements, and available documentation.
Step 3 Additional verification may be requested when necessary.
Step 4 Approved requests move into transportation coordination and scheduling.
