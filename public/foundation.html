How Assistance Works

The assistance process directs transportation resources toward eligible individuals while supporting responsible program administration.

Step 1Complete rider intake and eligibility application.
Step 2Submit eligibility details and program need statement.
Step 3Foundation reviews program fit, verification, and available support.
Step 4Approved ride assistance is coordinated and tracked.

Our Mission

Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation exists to reduce transportation-related hardship by helping connect people with care, services, work opportunities, education, and community resources that improve quality of life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from applicants, supporters, donors, partners, and community members.

What programs does the foundation support?Medical, senior, disability, veteran, employment, intake, provider verification, and ride tracking programs.
Who may be eligible?Eligibility may include seniors, people with disabilities, veterans, low-income applicants, Medicaid recipients, and others with transportation need.
How can I support the foundation?Donations can be made securely through the PayPal Giving Fund donation portal.
Is the foundation separate from Harvey Taxi?Yes. The foundation is a nonprofit-focused mission and remains separate from for-profit taxi platform operations.
