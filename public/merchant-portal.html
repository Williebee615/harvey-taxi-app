Import from a spreadsheet

Download the blank template and fill in one item per row, or start from the example file (replace its sample items with yours before importing).

How to fill in the file
ColumnNeeded?What to enter
nameRequiredThe item as customers will see it, 2 to 120 characters. Each name once per file.
priceRequiredThe price customers pay through Harvey Taxi, for example 4.99 ($0.01 to $1,000.00).
sectionOptionalA group such as Dairy or Bakery, up to 60 characters.
descriptionOptionalSize, flavor or other details, up to 500 characters.
availableOptionalyes if in stock now, no if not. Blank means yes.
substitutableOptionalyes if a driver may pick a similar item when it's out of stock. Blank means no.
  • In Excel or Google Sheets, save or download as CSV (UTF-8). Keep the header row exactly as in the template.
  • Up to 500 rows and 200 KB per file; up to 1,000 items per store.
  • An item whose name is already in your catalog is updated, not duplicated (capitals don't matter).
  • Items that might be on the excluded list (for example alcohol, tobacco or vape, CBD, gift cards) wait for a Harvey Taxi review before customers can see them. This isn't a rejection.
  • Always press Preview first. Nothing changes until you press Import, and lines with problems are listed by line number.

Prefer to email your spreadsheet? Send it to support@harveytaxiservice.com and Harvey Taxi staff can import it for you. The items then appear here, and the same review applies.
