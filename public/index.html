How It Works

A cleaner flow from sign-up to trip request, built around verification, payment readiness, dispatch, and support.

1. Create Your Account Riders and drivers begin with the correct sign-up path for their role.
2. Complete Verification Account review helps protect riders, drivers, and platform operations.
3. Request a Ride Approved riders can enter pickup, destination, contact details, and trip preferences.
4. Confirm Payment Readiness The platform is built to confirm payment authorization before dispatch.
5. Match with a Driver Dispatch logic helps connect the request with the right available driver.
6. Ride with Support Riders and drivers can use support tools throughout the platform experience.
