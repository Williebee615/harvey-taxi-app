Account & Data Controls

Delete Your Account

Harvey Taxi Service LLC — Rider & Driver Accounts

This page explains how to permanently delete your Harvey Taxi rider or driver account, operated by Harvey Taxi Service LLC, and what happens to your data when you do. It applies to Harvey Taxi accounts only — it is not affiliated with, and does not delete, an application submitted to the Harvey Transportation Assistance Foundation.

Option 1: Delete in the app

If you have the Harvey Taxi app installed, open it and go to Settings → Delete Account. You'll be asked to verify your identity with a one-time code sent to the phone number on your account, then confirm the deletion.

Option 2: Delete from the web (no app required)

You don't need the app installed to request deletion. Use the form below — it sends a one-time verification code by SMS to the phone number on your account, then submits the deletion request to the same backend system the app uses.

Identity verification required To protect your account, we require a one-time code texted to the phone number already on file before any deletion request is processed. We resolve your account from that verified phone number — no password, authentication token, or other sensitive credential is ever entered on this page or included in its URL.

What happens after you request deletion

  • Rider accounts: once your phone number is verified, your account is deleted immediately — your name, email, and phone number are permanently removed from your account record and it is deactivated right away.
  • Driver accounts: once your phone number is verified, your account access is disabled immediately. Because driver accounts carry documents and payout records that our compliance team reviews before finalizing, a member of our team then reviews the request and completes the permanent removal of your personal information. We do not currently commit to a fixed number of days for that review, but your ability to log in and drive is disabled as soon as your request is verified.

What data is deleted

Once your request is processed, the following personal information is permanently removed from your account record and from your past ride history:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your saved profile details and app preferences
  • Your name and phone number as they appeared on any past rides (replaced with a generic "Deleted User" / "Deleted Driver" placeholder)

What data is retained, and why

Deleting your account does not delete the underlying ride, payment, and safety records tied to it. We keep these records, with your personal identifying information removed, because we're required to for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, and safety reasons:

DataWhy we keep it
Ride records (route, fare, timestamps) Financial recordkeeping, tax reporting, and resolving disputes about a specific trip
Payment and earnings records Tax reporting obligations and accounting requirements for completed transactions and driver payouts
Audit logs and safety reports Fraud prevention, platform safety investigations, and legal or regulatory recordkeeping

These retained records are stripped of your name, email, and phone number as described above — they remain only as anonymized operational and financial records, not as an active, personally identifiable account.

This cannot be undone Once your account has been deleted, we cannot restore your profile, saved preferences, or the personal information removed from your ride history.

Questions about this process or your data? See our Privacy Policy or contact Harvey Taxi Support.
