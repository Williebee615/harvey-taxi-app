Bring your store to Harvey Taxi delivery

We're inviting a small group of Nashville-area restaurants, grocery and convenience stores to a delivery pilot. Apply below and our team will review your application and contact you.

Delivery isn't live yet. Riders can't order from stores today. Applying doesn't create an account, list your store or commit you to anything. We'll send the merchant agreement before anything goes live.

Pilot terms in brief

  • Commission: 12% of the item subtotal on completed orders only. Harvey Taxi covers card processing fees.
  • No monthly, subscription or fixed fees.
  • Payouts are sent weekly, every Monday, for the previous Monday–Sunday's completed orders. When the money reaches your bank depends on Stripe and your bank's processing times. Missing-item amounts confirmed after a payout are deducted from the next one.
  • You set your prices. If they're higher than in store, customers see "Prices may be higher than in store".
  • Missing items are refunded to the customer right away and deducted from your payout. You have 48 hours to dispute with photos; if you win, Harvey Taxi covers the refund.
  • Customers choose how each item is substituted if it's out of stock. You mark which items can be substituted.
  • Full terms are in the merchant agreement, which you'll review before joining.

Items that can't be sold through Harvey Taxi

  • Alcohol, tobacco and vape products
  • Cannabis, hemp and CBD products
  • Prescription medicines
  • Weapons and ammunition
  • Gift cards, prepaid cards and lottery tickets
  • Live animals
  • Hazardous materials
  • Oversized or highly fragile items, or items needing refrigerated or specialized transport
