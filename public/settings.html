Battery & Location

Balance battery life with live trip accuracy. During active rides, precise location may still be required for safety and dispatch.

Battery Optimizer Use fewer background checks when no ride, delivery, or driver mission is active.

Privacy & Security

Manage security controls and data privacy preferences.

Location Data Harvey Taxi stores trip-related location records to support ride history, safety, compliance, and dispute resolution.
Identity Verification Driver and rider verification records may be retained as required for platform security and fraud prevention.
Payment Security Harvey Taxi does not store full payment card numbers. Payments should remain tokenized through Stripe.
Account Protection Login monitoring and audit records help protect rider and driver accounts.
