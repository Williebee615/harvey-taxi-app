Settings

Manage Harvey Taxi app preferences, battery optimization, notifications, account type, and account deletion.

Account

Select whether this settings action is for a rider or driver account.

Battery Optimizer

Battery Optimizer reduces background tracking intensity when you are not actively on a ride or mission. For active trips, Harvey Taxi should keep live status accurate.

Enable Battery Optimizer Reduces background refresh, map updates, and location polling when idle.
Precise Location During Trips Recommended for riders and required for active driver missions.
Low Power Mode Warning Shows a reminder when battery saving may affect live tracking.

Delete Account

This sends a delete-account request to Harvey Taxi. For safety, the backend should deactivate the account first, preserve legally required trip/payment records, and remove unnecessary personal access data.
