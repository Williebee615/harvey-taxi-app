Battery & Location

These settings help balance battery life with live trip accuracy. During active rides, precise location may still be required for safety and dispatch accuracy.

Battery Optimizer Use fewer background checks when no ride, delivery, or driver mission is active.
Precise Location During Trips Allow high-accuracy location while requesting rides, driving, or completing missions.
Low Power Mode Reminders Show reminders if battery settings may interfere with active trip updates.
